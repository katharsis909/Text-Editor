### Chapter 1: The Monospace Canvas Engine
The editor renders multiple lines of text with subpixel monospace typography.
Each line is identified by its line index (1-based) shown in the left gutter.
You can navigate freely using Up, Down, Left, and Right arrow keys.
Press Enter to insert a new line anywhere in the middle!
Select text across multiple lines using mouse drag or Shift + Arrow keys.

### Technical Specifications
- Invariant 1: Original buffer is strictly immutable.
- Invariant 2: Add buffer is append-only for every keystroke.
- Invariant 3: Line breaks (\n) are first-class characters in the Piece Table.
- Invariant 4: Zero text reallocation on delete, undo, or redo operations.
